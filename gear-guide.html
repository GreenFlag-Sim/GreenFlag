04 / YOUR EQUIPMENT

Choose gear that fits you.

A wheel can add force feedback and a more direct feel, but you do not need one to learn. If you are thinking about buying, start with your console or PC, the games you play, and the space you have. There is no single “best” wheel for everyone.

Before you spend money.

Use this short check on any wheel you are considering, new or used.

Confirm the exact Xbox, PlayStation, or PC version and whether your game supports it.
Check what is included: base, steering wheel, pedals, power supply, cables, and clamp or stand.
Measure desk thickness and space. Make sure the pedals will stay in place.
Compare the full cost in Canadian dollars, including shipping, taxes, a stand, and accessories.
If buying used, ask to test steering and pedals and check that the correct power supply is included.
Check the seller’s return policy and warranty before ordering.

You can learn before you buy.

Try a few sessions with your controller first. Notice what you like and what feels limiting, then decide whether a wheel would make sim racing more fun for you. No brand or piece of gear is required to start.
